# Female-Centric Bathroom Redesign at Duke Kunshan University

This project is the culmination of my INFOSCI 104 Digital Design course, focusing on a Unity-based redesign of the bathrooms at Duke Kunshan University. The primary aim is to create a more female-centric environment by addressing specific concerns and enhancing functionality, rather than focusing on visual design.

## Key Features

- **Safety and Hygiene:** Prioritizing the well-being of users through thoughtful design choices, namely concerning menstrual product disposal.
- **Water Management:** Efficient water drainage for sustainability and comfort.
- **Lighting Adjustment:** Balancing lighting needs to optimize student experience.
- **Storage Optimization:** Intelligent storage solutions for better organization and convenience.

## Background

The redesign is informed by insights gathered from a comprehensive survey involving over 124 DKU students, ensuring that the project accurately reflects and addresses campus culture and concerns.
